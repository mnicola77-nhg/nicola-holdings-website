Company Overview

Nicola Holdings Group, LLC is a Florida-based, diversified government and commercial contracting company coordinating construction, facility, property, procurement, and specialty-trade requirements.

Delivery combines direct project coordination with qualified specialty subcontractors, licensed trades, suppliers, and strategic teaming partners, under one accountable point of contact.
