01 About

One accountable point of coordination for the built environment.

Nicola Holdings Group, LLC is a diversified government and commercial contracting company. We coordinate construction, facility, property, procurement, and specialty-trade work so that owners, agencies, and prime contractors have a single, responsive partner managing scope, schedule, and delivery.

Our model pairs direct project coordination with a network of qualified subcontractors, licensed specialty trades, suppliers, and strategic teaming partners. We match each requirement to the right delivery team, manage communication and documentation, and keep the work aligned to the contract.

Our focus areas span commercial and institutional facilities, public-sector requirements, property and REO support, and housing initiatives that serve veterans and their communities.

06 Veteran Housing

Veteran Housing & Property Support

Veteran housing is a priority growth area for Nicola Holdings Group. We intend to support organizations and initiatives that house veterans by applying our property, construction, maintenance, and facility capabilities to the homes and buildings they depend on.

  • Property sourcing support
  • Rehabilitation coordination
  • Property maintenance
  • Turnover readiness
  • Facility and property support

Nicola Holdings Group does not administer VA benefits or veteran housing programs. Our role is property, construction, and facility support for the organizations that do.
