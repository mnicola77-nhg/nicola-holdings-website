01 About

One accountable point of coordination for the built environment.

Nicola Holdings Group, LLC is a government and commercial contractor. We deliver construction, renovation, facilities, MEP, signage, and procurement scopes so that agencies, prime contractors, and facility owners have a single, responsive partner managing scope, schedule, and delivery.

Our model pairs direct project coordination with a network of qualified subcontractors, licensed specialty trades, suppliers, and strategic teaming partners. We match each requirement to the right delivery team, manage communication and documentation, and keep the work aligned to the contract.

Our focus is public-sector, institutional, and commercial facilities and the agencies and prime contractors that manage them. We also support property and REO requirements for government and institutional owners, and provide housing for veterans served by Supportive Services for Veteran Families (SSVF) programs.

05 Veteran Housing

Homes for veterans, ready for move-in.

Nicola Holdings Group is a housing provider to Supportive Services for Veteran Families (SSVF) grantees and the organizations that place veterans and their families in permanent housing. We source homes, renovate them to meet program habitability standards, and provide them move-in ready, with ongoing maintenance so each home stays ready for the family that lives there.

  • Move-in-ready homes for SSVF placements
  • Home sourcing and acquisition
  • Renovation to habitability standards
  • Ongoing maintenance and repairs
  • Turnover readiness between placements
  • One point of contact for SSVF case managers

Nicola Holdings Group is not an SSVF grantee and does not administer the SSVF program or VA benefits, or determine eligibility. We provide homes and property services to the grantees and organizations that place veterans.
